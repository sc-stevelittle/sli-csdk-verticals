The questions we hear most

What makes SitecoreAI's CMS different?

SitecoreAI connects a cloud-based web content management system, personalization, customer data, and AI in one platform. You can create, manage, and activate digital content across every experience, with AI already included and governance built in as core functionality.

Why do traditional content management systems struggle to support modern digital experiences?

Traditional CMS platforms like WordPress struggle because they tie content to a single presentation layer and rely on disconnected tools for delivery and optimization. A headless CMS solves the first problem by separating content from the front end, but most still leave personalization and optimization to bolt-on functionality, apps, or plugins added later. The result: harder content reuse, slower adaptation across channels, and a slower response to changing customer expectations.

Why do marketing teams struggle with content execution?

Most teams don't have a content problem, they have an execution problem. Time is lost to handoffs between marketers and developers, manual page building, market-by-market localization, and disconnected tools for testing and optimization. When content, data, and approvals live in separate systems, AI amplifies inconsistency instead of fixing it. SitecoreAI connects those systems in one governed platform, reducing cycle time from brief to publish.

How does a content management system impact SEO and AI search visibility?

Your CMS shapes how your content is discovered in search and AI-generated answers. SitecoreAI structures digital content, enriches metadata, and connects it to delivery and optimization. Powered by Scrunch, now a Sitecore company, SitecoreAI analyzes how AI search engines like ChatGPT and Gemini read, cite, and represent your content in their answers. Performance insights and priority recommendations appear in the same workspace, so you can improve your share of voice in AI answers alongside traditional search rankings, because customers now form opinions before they ever reach your website.

How does a CMS support the full content lifecycle?

A CMS supports the full content lifecycle by connecting how content is created, managed, and delivered. In SitecoreAI, content creation sits alongside production and delivery, with connections to assets, campaigns, and personalization workflows. That functionality lets the same brief power a landing page, an email, mobile apps, and a personalized recommendation for any use case in any format without rework.

How do you control and govern AI-generated content in a CMS?

Control comes from governance built into every step. SitecoreAI applies brand guardrails to every AI action across the content ecosystem, so content creation and content delivery stay consistent, compliant, and aligned across teams, channels, and experiences.

Can marketers edit pages visually and manage approval workflows in SitecoreAI CMS?

Yes. SitecoreAI CMS gives marketers a visual page editor with drag-and-drop components and live preview. Once components and workflows are configured, teams can make routine page updates without writing code and submit content for review. Authorized reviewers can compare versions, approve content, or return it for changes. Content governed by a workflow must complete its required approval stages before it becomes publishable.

What should buyers look for in a content management system for enterprise organizations?

Buyers should look for a platform that supports global teams, multiple channels, and complex governance requirements. Many types of content management systems, from Squarespace and Drupal to legacy CMS platforms, manage content but fail to connect it to delivery and performance, so you need flexible architecture, enterprise scalability, strong governance, and integration across your content lifecycle.

How does a CMS connect with other marketing tools?

Many CMS platforms stop at managing content. SitecoreAI connects the key features of CMS, digital asset management (DAM), Content Operations, Audience and Insights, and Conversion Optimization in one platform, so non-technical users can create, deliver, and improve experiences without a disconnected user experience.

Does Sitecore combine CMS and DAM?

Yes. SitecoreAI brings content management and digital asset management together in one connected platform, so marketers can create and publish experiences using governed, approved assets without moving between disconnected systems. CMS connects directly with DAM, audience intelligence, and optimization capabilities to help teams create, personalize, and deliver experiences faster. Sitecore customers have achieved an 80% gain in productivity and efficiency, while others have seen 3x higher visitor-to-lead conversion and 200% more website sales.

How often is SitecoreAI updated?

SitecoreAI is a cloud-native SaaS platform that delivers updates and new capabilities daily. Your team is always on the newest version automatically, no version upgrades, migration projects, or downtime windows.
